7. minimal.css

minimal.css is a stripped-down, typography-first theme for JoyCDN. It removes all decorative styling — no shadows, no gradients, no visual clutter — and focuses entirely on legibility, spacing, and clean structure. It’s ideal for writing-heavy sites, documentation, and minimalist dashboards.

📦 How to Use

To apply minimal.css to your site, include this in your HTML:

<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://cdn.joycode.co.uk/themes/minimal.css">

🎨 Theme Variables

  • Font: 'Helvetica Neue', sans-serif
  • Background: #ffffff
  • Text: #111111
  • Accent: #000000, hover: #333333
  • Card background: #f9f9f9
  • Border: #dddddd
  • Radius: 4px
  • Shadow: none

🔘 Buttons

Buttons are flat, black, and simple. They use white text, no borders, and a hover darkening effect. 

<button class="button">Minimal Button</button>

📦 Cards

Cards are light gray with a 1px border and small radius. There’s no shadow — just clean separation from the background.

📝 Forms

  • Inputs use 1px gray borders, white background, and black text
  • Focus state: border turns black
  • All fields are full-width with consistent padding and spacing

📢 Alerts

Alerts are subtle and text-focused:

  • .alert-info: Gray text
  • .alert-success: Dark green text
  • .alert-error: Deep red text
  • All alerts use a light gray background and a 4px left border

📐 Layout Helpers

  • .flex, .grid, .center, .hidden
  • All spacing and alignment utilities are preserved

🧪 Best Practices

  • Use semantic HTML and let the typography do the work
  • Pair with monochrome icons or minimalist illustrations
  • Use .card and .button for consistent layout
  • To apply this theme, manually update your <link> tag
