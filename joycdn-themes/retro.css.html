6. retro.css

retro.css is JoyCDN’s throwback theme — a bold, pixel-style design that channels the energy of early web interfaces. With monospaced fonts, uppercase headings, and thick black borders, it’s perfect for creative sites, developer portfolios, or anything that wants to feel fun and unapologetically old-school.

📦 How to Use

To apply retro.css to your site, include this in your HTML:

<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://cdn.joycode.co.uk/themes/retro.css">

🎨 Visual System

  • Font body: 'Courier New', monospace
  • Headings: 'Impact', sans-serif, all uppercase
  • Background: #fef3c7 (warm cream)
  • Text: #111 (deep gray)
  • Accent: #ff0066 (hot pink), hover: #cc0052
  • Card background: #fff8dc (cornsilk)
  • Border: #000, 2px solid
  • Shadow: 4px 4px 0 #000 (pixel-style drop)
  • Radius: 0 (no rounding)

🔘 Buttons

Buttons are bold, boxy, and unmistakable. They use the accent pink background, white text, thick black borders, and a hard shadow. On hover, the pink deepens. 

<button class="button">RETRO ACTION</button>

📦 Cards

Cards are styled with a cornsilk background, black border, and a chunky shadow. They’re perfect for retro-styled panels, quotes, or content blocks.

📝 Forms

Inputs, selects, and textareas use:

  • Monospaced font
  • 2px solid black borders
  • White background, black text
  • Focus outline: 2px dashed hot pink

📢 Alerts

Alert boxes are loud and proud:

  • .alert-info: #d0f0fd background, #003366 text
  • .alert-success: #d0fdd0 background, #006600 text
  • .alert-error: #fdd0d0 background, #990000 text

📐 Layout Helpers

  • .flex, .grid, .center, .hidden
  • All spacing and alignment utilities are preserved

🧪 Best Practices

  • Use uppercase headings for full retro effect
  • Pair with pixel art or 8-bit icons for visual consistency
  • Use .card and .button for maximum style impact
  • To apply this theme, manually update your <link> tag
