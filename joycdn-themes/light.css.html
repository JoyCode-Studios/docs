4. light.css

light.css is a bright, clean, and minimal theme in the JoyCDN collection. It’s ideal for content-focused websites, blogs, and documentation where clarity and readability are the top priorities. This theme emphasizes whitespace, soft contrast, and a neutral palette.

📦 How to Use

To apply light.css to your site, include this in your HTML:

<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://cdn.joycode.co.uk/themes/light.css">

🎨 Color Palette

The theme uses a bright neutral base with subtle accents:

  • --color-bg: Pure white or near-white background
  • --color-text: Deep gray or black for maximum legibility
  • --color-accent: Soft blue or green for links and buttons
  • --color-card: Slightly off-white for visual separation
  • --color-border: Light gray for outlines and dividers

🧱 Base Styling

The body uses a white background, system font stack, and generous padding. The layout is designed to feel open and breathable, with consistent spacing and clean lines.

🔘 Buttons

Buttons use the accent color for background and white text. Hover states darken the accent slightly. The button shape and sizing are consistent with other JoyCDN themes.

📦 Cards

Cards are rendered with a soft background, light border, and subtle shadow. They’re ideal for grouping content or highlighting sections.

📝 Forms

Form inputs are styled with light borders, rounded corners, and clear focus states. The theme ensures high contrast between text and background for accessibility.

📢 Alerts

Alert boxes follow the same structure as other themes, with color adjustments for light mode:

  • .alert-info: Light blue background, navy text
  • .alert-success: Pale green background, dark green text
  • .alert-error: Light red background, crimson text

📐 Layout Utilities

All layout helpers from joy.css are included:

  • .flex, .grid, .center, .hidden
  • Spacing and alignment utilities remain consistent

🧪 Best Practices

  • Use semantic HTML for optimal styling and accessibility
  • Use .card and .button for consistent UI
  • To switch to light.css, manually update the <link> tag in your HTML
